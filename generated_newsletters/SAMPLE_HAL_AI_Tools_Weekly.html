Tool Name: Perplexity AI
Category: AI Search & Research Freemium
📄
Overview:
Perplexity AI is an AI-powered answer engine that provides accurate, real-time answers to complex questions with cited sources. Unlike traditional search engines, it synthesises information across multiple sources into concise, readable summaries. It supports follow-up questions, file uploads, and deep research mode.
⭐
Key Features:
  • Real-time web search with cited, verifiable sources
  • Deep Research mode for multi-step, in-depth reports
  • File upload support for PDF/document Q&A
⚙️
How It Works:
Perplexity uses a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) architecture, combining large language models with live web search. When a query is submitted, it retrieves relevant web pages in real time, then synthesises the content into a coherent answer with inline citations. Users can refine results through follow-up prompts.
💼
Practical Applications:
Researchers use Perplexity to rapidly survey literature and compile annotated summaries. Business analysts rely on it for competitive intelligence and market research. Engineers use it to find up-to-date technical specifications, standards, and regulatory documents without manual web browsing.
👥
Potential HAL Use Case:
HAL engineers can use Perplexity AI to quickly survey global aerospace standards (MIL-SPEC, STANAG, ARP4754A) and retrieve the latest MRO best practices, reducing manual literature search time by 60–70%. It is particularly valuable for R&D teams tracking competitor aircraft programmes and emerging propulsion technologies.
💬 Sample Prompt:
"Summarise the latest revisions to ARP4761 and ARP4754A for safety assessment of civil aircraft systems, and highlight key differences from previous editions."

Tool Name: Notion AI
Category: AI Productivity & Notes Freemium
📄
Overview:
Notion AI is an integrated AI assistant built into the Notion workspace platform, enabling users to draft, summarise, translate, and analyse documents directly within their knowledge base. It connects to your existing Notion pages for context-aware answers and automations, making it a powerful tool for teams managing complex documentation.
⭐
Key Features:
  • AI writing, summarisation, and editing inline within Notion pages
  • Q&A over your entire Notion workspace with cited page references
  • Automated action items extraction from meeting notes
⚙️
How It Works:
Notion AI is embedded natively in the Notion editor. Users invoke it with a '/AI' command or by selecting text. It has access to the user's full Notion workspace (with permission), enabling it to retrieve context from linked documents and databases before generating responses. Outputs are inserted directly into the page for review.
💼
Practical Applications:
Engineering teams use Notion AI to auto-summarise lengthy design review documents and extract open action items. HR departments generate onboarding documents and policy summaries on demand. Project managers query the AI to track cross-team dependencies stored across multiple Notion databases.
👥
Potential HAL Use Case:
HAL's Design & Development departments can use Notion AI to build a centralised engineering knowledge base — summarising test reports, maintenance manuals, and RFP responses — and query it in natural language. This can accelerate document retrieval for LCA, ALH, and HTT programmes, reducing engineering search time significantly.
💬 Sample Prompt:
"Summarise all action items from last week's LCA Mk2 avionics integration review meeting notes and generate a priority-ranked task list for the systems team."
